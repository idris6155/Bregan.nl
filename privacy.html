How this website handles information.

This website is designed as a lightweight corporate and product-information site. It does not currently include advertising trackers or analytics scripts.

Information you send

Inquiry forms on this version prepare an email in your device’s email application. Information is sent to Bregan only when you choose to send that email.

02

Local preferences

The site stores limited preferences in your browser, such as language choice and products selected for comparison. These values are stored locally on your device.

03

External resources

Some fonts and images may be loaded from external providers or Bregan web resources. As with normal web requests, those providers may receive technical request data such as IP address and browser information.

04

Business communication

If you contact Bregan, the information you provide may be used to answer your inquiry, prepare technical or commercial information, and continue the related business communication.

05

Questions or requests

For questions about information submitted through the website or business communication, contact Bregan B.V. at info@bregan.nl.
